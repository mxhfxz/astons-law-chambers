About Astons Law Chambers

Astons Law Chambers is a London barrister practice acting as a criminal defence lawyer at every stage — police station, Magistrates' Court, Crown Court, and the Court of Appeal. The practice operates under the Bar Standards Board Public Access scheme and is also instructed through solicitor firms.

Practice

Astons Law Chambers acts in the Crown Court, Court of Appeal, Magistrates' Court, and tribunals. Police station attendance is provided 24/7. Instructions are accepted from members of the public under the Direct Access scheme and from solicitors in the usual way.

The practice has experience of prosecution work, having been instructed by the Crown Prosecution Service.

Geography

Based in central London. Acting UK-wide.
