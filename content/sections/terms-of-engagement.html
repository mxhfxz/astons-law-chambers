Description of services

The barristers at Astons Law Chambers practise in criminal defence. Every case is different; the key stages of a typical instruction are:

  • Initial consultation. Taking full instructions from you, in person or remotely.
  • Review of evidence. Analysing the Initial Details of the Prosecution Case, and the used and unused material.
  • Advice. A written or oral opinion on the strength of the evidence, the likely outcome, and the appropriate plea.
  • Drafting. Preparing legal arguments, skeleton arguments, or defence statements.
  • Representation. Advocacy at first appearances, case management hearings, trial, and sentencing.
