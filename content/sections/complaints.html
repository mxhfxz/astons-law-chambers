Complaints procedure

If you are unhappy with the service you have received from Astons Law Chambers, please raise it directly in the first instance.

How to complain

Complaints should be sent in writing to ghulam@astonslaw.com. Please include the nature of the complaint and any relevant dates.

How complaints are handled

The complaint will be acknowledged in writing within 7 calendar days of receipt. A full written response will follow within 28 calendar days of the acknowledgement. For complex matters this may extend to 8 weeks, with interim updates provided.

Legal Ombudsman

If Astons Law Chambers cannot resolve the complaint to your satisfaction, you may refer it to the Legal Ombudsman. The Legal Ombudsman accepts complaints within strict time limits — generally within six years of the act complained of, or within three years of you becoming aware of it, and within six months of Astons Law Chambers' final response.
