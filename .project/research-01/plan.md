## Goal

Produce three independent deep-research reports to inform the design and content strategy of the Astons Law Chambers website rebuild. The site is a barrister's practice site (Next.js + Tailwind + Vercel), targeting defendants in crisis. Primary KPIs are inbound phone calls and WhatsApp messages — specifically police station support, emergency support, and legal aid enquiries. No email funnels.
