## What not to do on this page

- No fabricated facts. The fabrication-blocked items in memory are:
  BSB number, Inn, year of call, named cases, named partners, training
  completion date, education credentials. Until each is confirmed in
  session, the page does not assert any of them.
- No portrait. Memory rule.
- No testimonials, no review-count widgets. Memory rule.
- No email address. Memory rule.
- No alternating tonal banding. The page sits on white; the only dark
  section is the contact aside.
- No marketing-speak. No "the right barrister for...", no rhetorical
  questions, no triadic phrasings.
- No specific statutory section numbers, sentencing figures, or case
  citations unless verified for 2026.
