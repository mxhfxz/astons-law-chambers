  Emergency state (custody, hearing imminent) and deliberation state (case in
  the diary, comparing barristers).

  nobody arrives unaware.
- **Trust:** low by default for legal services. Proof beats flourish.

  structures, no "right barrister" self-qualifying, no AI prose patterns, no
