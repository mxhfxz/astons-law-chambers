Home / Direct access

Direct access.

Direct access — sometimes called public access — is the route that lets a member of the public instruct a barrister without first going through a solicitor.

Astons Law Chambers takes direct instructions for the majority of its caseload. Initial contact is by phone or WhatsApp. From there, the barrister will tell you whether direct access is suitable for your matter, or whether a solicitor's involvement is needed before instructions can be accepted.

When direct access works

  • Police station representation — including out-of-hours callouts.
  • Magistrates' and Crown Court hearings where evidence is straightforward and documentary preparation is contained.
  • Motoring matters where the issue is identified early.
  • Immigration applications and appeals where the paperwork is manageable directly.
  • Advice on the merits of an investigation, charge, or appeal, before deciding next steps.

When a solicitor is needed

  • Cases requiring sustained investigative work, witness management, or document handling at scale.
  • Matters where legal aid is the right funding route — legal aid is administered through partner solicitor firms.
  • Some complex Crown Court trials, depending on the nature of the evidence and the volume of disclosure.

Where a solicitor is needed, Astons Law Chambers will refer to a firm it works with and can continue to be instructed as counsel.

Direct access training

Direct access work is regulated under the Bar Standards Board's Public Access scheme. 🚩 Confirm A barrister taking direct instructions must hold the required Public Access training. Astons Law Chambers operates under this scheme.

Legal aid

Legal aid funding is administered through solicitor firms. Where a case qualifies, Astons Law Chambers refers to a partner firm and can continue to be instructed as counsel. Eligibility depends on the type of case and the client's means.

First call

The initial conversation is free of charge. There is no duration framing and no booking required — phone, WhatsApp, or a callback through Cal.com.

How to instruct

  1. Call or message with a short description of the matter.
  2. The barrister will confirm whether direct access is appropriate.
  3. A client care letter setting out scope, fees, and VAT is issued before any work begins.
  4. Funds are taken on account against the agreed scope.

Complaints.

Astons Law Chambers takes complaints seriously and aims to resolve them quickly and fairly.

Internal procedure

A complaint may be made in writing to the chambers. Astons Law Chambers will acknowledge the complaint and respond within a defined timeframe. 🚩 Response window

Legal Ombudsman

If the matter is not resolved internally, a complaint may be referred to the Legal Ombudsman. Time limits apply: ordinarily within six years of the act or omission, or three years of when the issue should reasonably have been known.
